Privacy policy

Android games by RG - Rezix's Games Company · Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy applies to all games published on Google Play by RG - Rezix's Games Company ("we"). Our principle: collect the bare minimum. Our games require no account, no email, no password and no name.

1. Controller and contact

RG - Rezix's Games Company. For any question or request about your data: b.deflandre.pro@gmail.com.

2. What we keep ourselves: nothing

We have no server and no database. We do not receive, store or sell any personal data about you.

3. What stays on your phone

Your scores, progress, settings (sound, vibration), daily challenge streak and consent choice are stored only on your device, in the game's private storage. If Android backup is enabled, Google may copy this data to your Google account to restore it on a new phone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

4. Google services used by our games

To fund free games and improve them, our games use services from Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, which process some data directly, under their own terms:

ServiceWhyData processed by Google
Google AdMob Show ads (banner, videos you choose to watch) Device advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interactions. Personalized ads only if you agree.
Firebase Analytics Anonymous usage statistics (games played, scores, ads viewed) Random installation ID, device model, approximate country, game events. Never the advertising ID. Subject to your consent choice; kept 2 months.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports, to fix bugs Technical information (version, model, error trace) and a random installation ID. No data about you.
Firebase Remote Config Adjust the game remotely (ad frequency) Random installation ID, game version.

Our games also download the list of our other games from this website (hosted by GitHub); as with any web page, GitHub then sees the device's IP address.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, how Google uses data from partner apps, Firebase privacy, GitHub privacy statement.

5. Your consent

On first launch, in the European Union, a consent form (Google's certified tool) asks for your choice before any ad is shown. Statistics record nothing before that choice. If you decline, the game works normally, with non-personalized ads. You can change your mind at any time: Settings → Privacy options.

6. Your rights

  • Delete your data: Settings → Erase my data erases everything on the phone and resets your statistics ID. Uninstalling the game also deletes everything.
  • Change your consent: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • To exercise your rights (access, correction, deletion, objection) or ask a question: b.deflandre.pro@gmail.com. Since we hold no data about you, requests about data processed by Google should also be made to Google.
  • EU residents may lodge a complaint with their data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).

7. Transfers outside the European Union

Google and GitHub may process data in the United States, under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (Data Privacy Framework, standard contractual clauses).

8. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect any data about them.

9. Changes

This policy may change (new game, new service). The date at the top shows the last update.
